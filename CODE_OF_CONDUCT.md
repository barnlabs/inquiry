# Community code of conduct

BarnLabs wants Inquiry to be useful to students, researchers, engineers, clinicians, public servants, and independent investigators without making participation hostile or unsafe.

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented. Critique claims and code rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, solicit credentials, or use project spaces to target a private person. Do not post patient data, sealed records, assessment answers, confidential investigations, or material you are not authorized to share.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect people, lawful research, or the project. Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@barnlabs.net`; include the minimum information needed and do not send secrets.

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) while keeping this local policy concise and specific to Inquiry's research risks.
