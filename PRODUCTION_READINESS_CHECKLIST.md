## Contributor handoff

Assign one ID only. The implementer gets the row, exact source/tests, and forbidden actions. Two fresh, context-isolated adversarial reviewers each get the row, diff, evidence, and failure probes; each reopens the source, reruns the smallest relevant check, and investigates a plausible wrong path. A useful checklist can pass documentation review while the product remains `REWORK`; do not mark the product production-ready until every applicable release row is green.
